#### How I source

- **GitHub first.** Commits, PRs and issues tell me who actually built the thing, not just who lists it.
- **Where the niche lives.** When Ray was brand new, the people were in the Ray Slack and at Ray Summit, so that's where I went.
- **AI-assisted pipelines.** Claude, Clay and Juicebox for research and enrichment; Boolean and X-ray search for precision.
- **Engineering partnership.** I define requirements with CTOs and hiring managers, calibrate the bar, and pitch complex roles in a way engineers respect.
